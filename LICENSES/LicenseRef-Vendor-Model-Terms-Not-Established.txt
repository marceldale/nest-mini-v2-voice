Vendor 3D models and footprints: terms not established
======================================================

This is not a licence. It is a placeholder that records an honest gap, so that the
repository does not claim rights it does not have.

The files under `parts/` are 3D models (STEP, OBJ, STL) and footprints for purchased
components. They came from the component manufacturers themselves and from library
portals that redistribute manufacturer data. For none of them do we hold a licence
document, and most vendors publish such models without stating any terms at all.

What that means in practice:

* We grant no rights in these files. Our own licences (CERN-OHL-S-2.0 for the hardware,
  MIT for tools and documentation) do not extend to them.
* They are kept in the repository because the board references them 30 times and the
  3D view and the mechanical checks do not work without them. They carry no electrical
  or geometric information that is not already in the footprints.
* If you redistribute this repository, or any derivative of it, check this directory
  against your own requirements first. If you need a repository that is clean in this
  respect, delete `parts/` and fetch the models again from each manufacturer; the board
  files will open with missing 3D models and nothing else will change.
* Nothing in `parts/` is needed to fabricate or assemble the board. The Gerbers, the
  drill files, the BOM and the CPL are complete without it.

Affected components, by the file names in `parts/`: Molex 1054500101 (USB-C), TE
1775443-2, Johanson 2450AT18A100E (antenna), Murata BLM18PG121SN1D and BLM18PG300SN1D
(ferrite beads), CHS-01TA and the remaining entries of that directory.


The same applies to part of `lib_lokal/`
=======================================

`lib_lokal/` holds the symbol and footprint libraries the design references. Most of it is
either a trimmed excerpt from the KiCad standard libraries (CC-BY-SA-4.0, attributed, see
LICENSES/CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt) or work that came with the upstream design. But three groups in
it have the same gap as `parts/`:

* **`Onju.pretty`** — 23 footprints that arrived with the upstream design. **Ten of them
  carry a SnapEDA or EasyEDA note** in the file; the other thirteen carry none.
* **`New_Library.kicad_sym` and `FH34SRJ-10S-0.5SH_50_.kicad_sym`** — SnapEDA symbols.
  `New_Library` holds 58 of the symbol instances in this design, so it cannot simply be
  dropped.
* **`Seeed_libadl`, `Seeed_libdb`, `libdb`, `reSpeaker`, `0_Seeed_Misc`** — symbols from
  Seeed's reSpeaker project, 32 instances in total.

These files reach us through the upstream repository, which publishes them under
CERN-OHL-S v2, and we pass them on unchanged. We hold no licence document for the parts that
originally came from a library portal, so **we cannot warrant their terms** and we grant no
rights in them beyond passing on what we received.

Unlike `parts/`, this directory **cannot** simply be deleted: without it the design does not
open. If you need a repository that is clean in this respect, the symbols and footprints
concerned have to be redrawn from the manufacturers' datasheets. The pad geometry is in the
board file either way, so the board itself does not depend on the library files for
fabrication.
